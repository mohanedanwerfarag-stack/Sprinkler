# 🚜 Agricultural Impact Sprinkler - SolidWorks CAD Project

A detailed 3D parametric CAD model and complete mechanical assembly of an **Agricultural Impact Sprinkler**, designed and structured using **SolidWorks**. This project breaks down a fully functional irrigation system into its individual components, showcasing advanced multi-part assembly mates, kinematic mechanisms, and fluid pathway design.

---

## 🛠️ Project Overview & Design Features

The sprinkler is engineered to simulate realistic agricultural irrigation behavior, featuring:
* **Fluid Flow Architecture:** Internal body and base channels optimized for smooth fluid transfer and minimal pressure drop[cite: 3].
* **Oscillating Impact Mechanism:** Spring-loaded swingarm (`Swingarm`, `Engage Spring`, and `Engage Lever`) that uses the kinetic energy of the water jet to drive continuous, uniform rotation[cite: 3].
* **Adjustable Coverage:** Includes specialized rotation and spray angle adjustment rings to switch between partial-circle and full 360-degree watering[cite: 3].
* **Wear & Friction Reduction:** Integrated upper and lower bearings, washers, and sealing gaskets to ensure smooth rotational longevity[cite: 3].
